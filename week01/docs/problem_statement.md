# Draft problem statement

Provisional Week 1 draft; validate with operators and more representative data.

Use the same four questions from the lecture, then add the two that follow.

- **User:** The shift supervisor, working with the maintenance lead during shift handover.
- **Decision:** Which production line should receive priority for a human inspection on the next shift; the signal will not automatically stop or adjust equipment.
- **Evidence:** Hourly, line-level records of units produced, downtime, energy use, rejected units, and motor temperature, together with data-quality checks and agreed operating thresholds. The current teaching sample is simulated and covers only one day.
- **Consequence:** A false alarm can divert maintenance time from higher-priority work; a missed warning can contribute to disruption or quality loss.
- **Desired value:** Help staff prioritize timely inspections and reduce avoidable downtime or rejects while keeping the decision with people.
- **One assumption that must be checked:** Hourly measurements are accurate and comparable across lines. Confirm the measurement definitions, timestamps, and sensor records with plant staff before relying on comparisons.
